# Two games until you know

In MMA the same week can hold a wrestler round and a striker round. Keep them separate so the reps stay clean.

Wrestler round: partner shoots. You sprawl, whizzer, and stand, or frame and angle off the fence. Head position stays tight. Do not grab the fence.

Striker round: partner jabs and kicks. You return the jab to the guard, check with the shin, and exit on an angle. Hands come home.

One theme per round. A coach watches before it goes live. No unsupervised hard sparring. If the card is under amateur rules, elbows and knees to the head stay out of the drill.
