# Wrestling fundamentals

Stance first. Hips low, back flat enough to see the opponent's hips, hands in front, feet moving. A shot from a tall, still stance is a gift. Hand-fight to move their hands, then change levels. The penetration step goes to the space between their feet or to the lead leg, not past them into a dive.

On a double leg, the head stays up and tight to the side, the hands lock behind the knees or at the hips, and you finish by driving through a square stance or by running the corner. Do not leave your knees on the mat and your head outside. A sprawl is hips down, chest on the upper back, and legs back. Then whizzer or crossface and come back to your feet.

Folkstyle scores a takedown, an escape, a reversal, and near-fall. Freestyle scores exposure and does not use the same riding game. Greco-Roman does not allow attacks below the waist. Do not teach a leg attack as Greco. No strikes in any of these.

Drill the shot to a finish line on the wall: level change, contact, finish on the feet. Cooperative, then the partner sprawls at half speed. A coach watches before it is live. Do not dive on a bad shot to prove effort.
