# Sambo fundamentals

Sport sambo uses a jacket, shorts, and wrestling shoes. The grip should move the person, not hang on the cloth. A hip throw starts with the belt or the sleeve loading them onto your hip, your knees bent, and a turn that finishes. Stalling with two hands on the jacket and no attack gives up the exchange.

Sport sambo scores throws and allows arm locks and many leg locks, including attacks that IJF judo does not allow. It does not allow chokes, and it does not allow strikes. Combat sambo adds strikes and chokes. Do not mix the two in one answer. A heel hook or a knee bar that is legal in sport sambo can be illegal in IBJJF gi. Name the rule set.

The finish of a throw still needs a partner who can fall. Drill the entry to the hip without the throw, then a slow throw onto a mat, then a light resistive entry. Leg locks are a coach-on-the-mat subject: control the knee line, apply slowly, and let go on the tap. Do not teach a ripping finish.

Check FIAS rules for the event. Local clubs often blend sambo with judo or wrestling. Ask which rule set the class is using before you change the grip.
