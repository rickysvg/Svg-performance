# Karate fundamentals

A reverse punch, gyaku zuki, is a hip punch with a short path. The front knee stays stacked over the front foot. The back hip turns in. The fist travels straight and returns. Leaning the head over the front knee to add reach drops the rear hand and kills the hip. The fist starts and ends in a position you can guard from. A long chamber out to the side is a tell and a slow path.

Stance in kumite is lighter than a deep front stance held for a kihon drill. You need to step. The lead hand occupies the center. The rear hand is the punch. Kicks chamber and return. A kick that stays out is a catch.

WKF kumite scores controlled techniques to legal targets. Face contact is not one rule for every age and division. Excessive contact is a penalty, and so is a wild technique that misses by being uncontrolled. Grabbing and uncontrolled throws are limited. Check the current WKF competition rules for the category.

Drill gyaku zuki in a mirror: ten reps where the head stays over the hips and the rear hand returns. Then a partner with a target pad at slow speed. Point sparring is a class, not a solo bout.
