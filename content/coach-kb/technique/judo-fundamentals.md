# Judo fundamentals

Judo throws are kuzushi, tsukuri, kake: break balance, fit in, finish. Osoto gari is not a kick to the heel. You drive the partner onto the leg you are going to reap, chest to chest, head committed past them, then the leg reaps that weighted leg. If you swing the leg and hope, they step off and you are the one falling.

Seoi and hip throws need the hips lower than theirs and a sleeve or lapel that actually moves them. Pulling the sleeve without moving your own feet is not an entry. On the ground, hold only while the attack is progressing. Stalling in newaza is penalized.

Under current IJF contest rules, a direct hand grab on the opponent's leg in standing is a penalty. Do not teach a morote gari as IJF judo. Ippon needs speed, force, and largely flat impact, with control. A soft tip is not ippon. Rules change. Check the current IJF sport rules before a tournament.

Practice the breakfall before the throw. Drill osoto as a fit-in without the reap, then a slow reap onto a crash pad, then a cooperative throw. A coach watches the head position. No standing leg grabs in an IJF rules practice unless the coach has set a different ruleset on purpose.
