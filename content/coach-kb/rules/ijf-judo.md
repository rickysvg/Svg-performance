# IJF judo rules, coaching version

Under current IJF contest rules, a direct hand grab on the opponent's leg during standing judo is penalized. Do not answer "can I grab the legs?" with a setup that hides a leg grab. Traditional judo still contains leg attacks. The contest rule is the one that matters on an IJF mat. Some clubs train leg grabs on purpose under a different rule set. Name which mat you are on.

Scores: ippon ends the match and needs a throw with force, speed, and largely flat impact, landed with control. Waza-ari is a real score and two of them can make ippon under the current system. Groundwork must be progressing. Stalling draws a penalty. Golden score is used when the match is tied. Exact penalty tables change. Check the current IJF sport and organization rules before a competition.

Safety: breakfalls are part of the technique. A throw practiced at full speed on a partner who does not know how to fall is how people get hurt. No medical advice here.
