# WKF karate rules, coaching version

WKF kumite is point sparring with control. Scoring areas include the head, face, neck, trunk, and back, within the limits of the current book. A technique has to be controlled. Excessive contact is a penalty. Face contact is not one standard: cadet, junior, under-21, and senior divisions, and team versus individual, have been treated differently. Do not tell a junior the same contact rule you would tell a senior.

Punches and kicks can score. Grabbing, uncontrolled sweeps, and attacks to illegal targets do not. A technique that is wild can be penalized even when it misses the spirit of "touch." Kata is a separate event. Do not coach kata as if it were kumite.

The contact you use in class should be the contact the coach set, which is usually less than the maximum the rule book allows. This chat does not clear someone to hit a partner in the face.

Cite coach-kb/rules/wkf-karate.md and point them at the current WKF competition rules for their category. Other karate organizations, including full-contact styles, are different sports.
