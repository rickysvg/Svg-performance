# Kickboxing, taekwondo, sambo, boxing, wrestling

Kickboxing: most K-1, Glory, and WAKO sets ban elbows. The clinch is short or absent. Knees depend on the set. Low-kick rules and point-fighting above-the-waist rules are different. A low kick that is the whole game in one set can be illegal in another. Check the promoter.

World Taekwondo: kicks score, head kicks and turning kicks score more, a punch to the trunk can score, a punch to the head does not. Elbows and knees are not WT techniques. Point values move. Check the current WT rules. ITF and other taekwondo bodies are not WT.

Sambo: sport sambo allows throws and many arm and leg locks, and it bans chokes and strikes. Combat sambo allows strikes and chokes on top of the throwing game. Check FIAS for the event. Do not export a combat-sambo strike into a sport-sambo practice.

Boxing: elbows and holding are fouls. The referee breaks the clinch. Amateur and pro differ on headgear, round length, and the standing eight. Wrestling: folkstyle, freestyle, and Greco-Roman are three rule sets. Greco does not allow attacks below the waist. No strikes.

Cite coach-kb/rules/kickboxing-and-others.md when the question is one of these sports, and name the body that wrote the rules.
