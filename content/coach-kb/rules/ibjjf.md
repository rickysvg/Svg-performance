# IBJJF rules, coaching version

IBJJF is the common reference for gi and no-gi jiu-jitsu. It is not the only rule set. ADCC, submission-only, and local opens differ. Say which book you mean.

In the gi, heel hooks are illegal at every belt. Knee reaping is illegal. Slams are illegal. Neck cranks are illegal. Points reward a takedown, a sweep, knee-on-belly, a guard pass, mount, and back control. Advantages cover near scores. Pulling guard is a choice with a cost at some levels. Check the current IBJJF rule book for the point values and for your belt, because no-gi leg locks are not the same as gi leg locks. Heel hooks in no-gi are a brown-and-black subject in the IBJJF adult divisions and are not a white-belt technique. Do not teach a heel hook from this note.

A legal guard pass still needs the control the rule book describes, not a scramble you call a pass. Spine and neck pressure that would be a catch-wrestling crank is a foul here.

Safety: tap early, let go on the tap, and do not hold a submission through a verbal tap because you did not feel the hand. Coaching note only. Cite coach-kb/rules/ibjjf.md and tell the member to read the current book for their division.
