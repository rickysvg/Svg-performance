# Amateur MMA rules

Amateur MMA is not pro MMA with headgear. ABC amateur rules and IMMAF ban elbow and forearm strikes of any kind, and they ban knees to the head. A forearm frame that pushes is not the same thing as an elbow strike, and you should not tell an amateur that a pro elbow is legal for them.

IMMAF's fouls list also keeps heel hooks illegal and bans twisters, neck cranks, can-openers, and other submissions that pressure the neck or spine. Since 2025, IMMAF has treated athletes who competed in MMA that allowed elbows or knees to the head as ineligible, with only a limited grace period for older bouts. That is an eligibility rule, not a technique cue. Check IMMAF before you enter.

Local amateur shows vary. Some allow a limited clinch. Some ban knees entirely. Some use shorter rounds. Do not export one city's amateur rules to another city. The phrase to use is: check your commission or promotion.

If a member asks whether they can knee a grounded opponent in the head, the answer for amateur MMA is no. The answer under the 2024 pro Unified Rules is also no while that opponent is grounded. The definitions of grounded still differ by commission.
