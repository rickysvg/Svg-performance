# In-season

In-season means they are competing often enough that the next camp is never far. Strength is maintained, not built from zero. One or two hard exposures on a main lift, inside 4 sets, at the RIR the plan already uses, is enough to keep the load. Do not test a new max the week of a fight. Accessories stay at 3 sets or fewer and do not become a bodybuilding block.

Skill follows the competition calendar. The weeks near a fight look like the taper. The weeks between fights can look like a short base: technical volume, a little strength, conditioning that matches the round. Hard sparring is a coach's session with a job, not an open invitation to get hit twice a week because the member is anxious.

If they have a fight every few months, the mistake is living in peak all year. Peak is short. Between fights they need enough work to get better and enough rest to show up healthy. Head symptoms and joint pain that changes movement stop the plan. No extra sessions to pay for a loss. The next scheduled week is the week.
