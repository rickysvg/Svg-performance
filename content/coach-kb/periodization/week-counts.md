# Weeks until the fight

Count the weeks from today to the fight date the member named, or to the fight date already saved on their camp. If they only name a month, plan to the start of that month. Say the number. Do not invent a date they did not give and do not have on file.

About 12 weeks or more: base and strength first, then specific prep, then a short peak, then fight week. Base keeps skill volume, lifts, and aerobic rounds they can repeat. Sparring stays technical. Specific prep makes the work look like the fight and the round length. Peak cuts volume and keeps speed. Fight week is short and sharp. No new techniques that week.

About 6 to 8 weeks: skip a long base. Start in specific prep, then peak, then fight week. Do not cram a 16-week template into a short camp.

About 1 to 3 weeks: this is peak and fight week. Ease hard sparring. Do not add a new max or a new technique.

The word "focus" in "what should I focus on" is the training priority, not a nerves problem, when a fight date or a camp is in the question.
