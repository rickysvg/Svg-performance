# Fight camp phases

A camp is base, then build, then peak, then a taper. The app's camp notes use those names. Do not invent a fight date. If the member context has a camp, use that phase and that date. If it does not, speak in general and say you do not have their date.

Base: aerobic work they can repeat, skill volume, and strength that still progresses. Sparring is technical and limited. This is not the week to cut weight fast.

Build: the skill gets more specific to the opponent and the rule set. Strength stays, usually with less chasing of new loads. Conditioning looks more like the fight rounds. Hard sparring, if a coach is running it, has a job and a stop time.

Peak: volume comes down. Speed stays. Hard sparring eases off so they arrive sharp instead of sore. New techniques stop. The game plan is the one they already have.

None of these phases includes a dehydration protocol. Weight, if it has to move, moved in base and build at about 1% of body weight per week or slower. Fight week is the taper note, not a sauna plan.

A beginner with no fight does not get this template. They get the beginner week.
