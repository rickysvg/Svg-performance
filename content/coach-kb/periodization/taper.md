# Taper and fight week

Fight week is a volume cut, not a personality change. Drop the amount of work. Keep a little speed so the nervous system remembers the sport: short pad rounds, sharp technical entries, easy movement. Hard sparring is done. A new technique is a bad idea. A max lift is a bad idea.

A useful picture is about a fifth less training volume than the week before, with the hard pieces removed first. Sleep and meals get more boring and more consistent. The member still trains. They do not lie down for seven days and call it a taper.

Weight: if they are near the class, this is not the week to invent a large cut. The gradual loss already happened. This chat will not write a sauna, a sweat suit, a diuretic, or a water load. A coach or a clinician runs any fight-week weight plan in person. Hydration follows the commission's rules for that weigh-in.

Nerves in fight week are the mindset lane: a routine, a longer exhale, one cue. The training answer stays short. Cite the camp phase if the member context has one. If it does not, do not invent the date.
