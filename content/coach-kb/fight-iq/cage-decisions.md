# Cage decisions

The fence changes the geometry. Being pinned square is the bad position. The decision is underhook, head position, and an angle, in that order when you can get them. Frame the jaw to turn the head, step the hips out, and face them. Grabbing the fence with fingers or toes is a foul. Do not put "grab the fence to stop the shot" in a game plan.

If you are the one pressing, you use the fence to keep them from circling, not as a weapon. Wrist control, head on their head, and a trip or a strike that the rule set allows. Pro Unified Rules and amateur rules do not allow the same strikes. Knees to a grounded head are out. Elbows are an amateur foul in most amateur MMA.

If-then: if they circle, cut the cage with your feet first. If they clinch, win the inside position. If you are down, frame and wall-walk instead of accepting a still guard on the fence with your back flat.

Drill short positional rounds on the wall with a coach. The constraint is "angle off or hold the underhook." This is tactics. Keep the feelings conversation in the mindset notes.
