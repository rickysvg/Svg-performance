# Unknown opponent

When the opponent is the winner of a card that has not happened, do not pretend you know the style. Prepare two likely games until the fight is on tape.

Wrestler path: hands on the head, hips back, sprawl, whizzer, stand up or a clinch you chose. If you are on the fence, frame the head, win an underhook, and angle out.

Striker path: jab returns home, check the kick, answer with one shot, exit on an angle. Do not walk straight back.

Split skill time between those paths. After the event, watch the winner once. Write one primary path and one backup. Drop the path that does not match the tape. A coach watches the drill. No unsupervised hard sparring.

If the rule set bans a weapon, that weapon is not in either path. Amateur MMA usually has no elbows and no knees to the head. Name the commission when legality matters. For El Paso, that check includes Texas TDLR.
