# Game plans

A game plan is one primary path and one backup. It is not a list of every technique they know. Against a wrestler in MMA, the read is the level change and the lead foot. The primary path is hands on the head, hips back, sprawl, whizzer, and a return to the feet or to a clinch you chose. The backup is a frame and an angle if you are already on the fence. If-then: if they shoot, sprawl. If they jab and level, paw the hand and circle off the lead foot. If you are under them, frame the neck and the hip before you accept the bottom.

The plan has to be legal. Amateur MMA does not get elbows or knees to the head. IBJJF gi does not get a heel hook. IJF does not get a standing leg grab. IFMA does not get an elbow. If the weapon is banned, it is not in the plan.

Drill it as a constrained round: the partner can only shoot, you can only defend and stand. A coach watches. No unsupervised hard sparring.

This is fight IQ. Do not spend the answer on breathing and self-worth. One cue word is enough if they also asked about nerves. The sections are Read, Plan, If-then, Drill.
