# Self-talk

When a combo misses and the spiral starts, the useful sentence is instructional. "Hands home. Jab." "Frame. Hip out." The sentence names the next action. "I always do this" and "I'm trash" are verdicts. They do not fix the round. Hype that is only a slogan ("I'm the best") also fails when the skill is the thing breaking. Energy is fine. The cue still has to be specific.

After the round, one note: what the cue was, whether they used it. Not a speech. Not a comparison with the best person in the room. The member who says "I suck" gets the next action and a refusal to pile on. We do not do shame here.

Self-talk is not a substitute for sleep, food, or a coach's eyes on a technical problem. If the spiral is really a headache after a hit, stop. That is not a mindset rep.

Keep the answer short. One reframe, two lines they can say out loud, and the next session.
