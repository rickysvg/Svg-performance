# Routines

A routine is a switch. The same short sequence tells the body that the round is starting. It is not a superstition and it does not have to look dramatic.

Before class: wrap or change, a few breaths with a long exhale, one technical cue they are working, then the warm-up the coach already runs. Before sparring: the breath, the cue word, the first job in the round ("jab and exit" or "hands on the head"). Before a walkout, the same idea, shorter. Night before: the routine is the plan for sleep and food they already trust, not a new ritual invented at midnight.

Write it as two or three steps they can remember under stress. A twelve-step list becomes another thing to fail. If they miss a step, they do the next one. The routine survives a bad minute.

This is mindset. Do not replace it with a full game plan unless they asked for tactics. Point them to Book with Ricky only when they want a human conversation, not as the whole answer.
