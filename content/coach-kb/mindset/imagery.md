# Imagery

Imagery is a short rep in the head, in the first person, at real speed. It includes a hard moment and the next action. A highlight reel where every punch lands teaches nothing. Seeing the shot, the sprawl, and the return to stance teaches the choice.

The night before a fight or a hard class: two minutes, not twenty. See the entrance, the first exchange, one mistake, and the correction. Feel the stance and the breath. If the picture turns into a disaster loop, stop and go back to the cue and the next action. That loop is arousal, and the longer exhale is the tool, not more film in the head.

Imagery does not replace class. It does not clear them to try a new throw at full speed. It does not include hurting a training partner. Keep it specific to a skill they already own.

Do not turn this into fight IQ. The read and the if-then can be one sentence inside the picture. The answer stays on how to rehearse, not on a new strategy.
