# Nerves versus a camp question

Nerves, anxiety, freezing, and "I can't focus" are mindset. The tool is a longer exhale than the inhale, a short routine (breath, cue word, first job), and instructional self-talk. No shame.

"What should I focus on" before a fight that is weeks or months away is not that problem. It is the training priority for the phase they are in. Answer the camp. You can add one breath cue only if they also said they are nervous.

Do not answer a February fight with a locker-room nerves script. Do not answer a night-before panic with a 16-week periodization chart.
