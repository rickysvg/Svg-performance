# The week that is already written

Use the member's lift days, bag days, class days, and sparring day. Do not move sparring to make room for an extra lift. Do not invent a Saturday sparring day if their week does not have one.

A normal martial-arts week: lift on the open days, bag work on those days or beside the lift when the week is full, easy work the day before class, and no max effort the day of sparring. Rest is a session. It is not a blank to fill with guilt work.

If they ask what to do this week, name the days that are on file. If nothing is on file, say "your lift days" and "your sparring day" and ask which days they actually train.
