# Gradual weight making

Start weeks out. Lose about 1% of body weight per week or slower. Faster than that is a red line for this coach. The loss comes from food: a bit less overall, protein kept in the range of about 0.7 to 1.0 grams per pound of walking-around weight, training kept in the plan. Pick a class near walking weight. An amateur who needs a large cut to make a class is in the wrong class.

Hydration stays normal until the commission's rules for that weigh-in say otherwise. This chat does not write the exception. A coach or a clinician runs fight week in person.

Why water cuts are dangerous, if they ask: you lose blood volume, heart rate climbs, temperature control gets worse, skill and thinking get worse, and heat illness becomes more likely. The brain does not like a crash fluid loss. Explain that. Do not explain how to do it.

Red lines, no protocols and no numbers for these: sauna suits, sweat suits, garbage bags, diuretics, laxatives, spitting, water loading, and "no water for" plans. A question about how far out to start is allowed and should be answered with the gradual path.
