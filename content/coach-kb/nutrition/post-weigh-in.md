# After the weigh-in

When the scale is done, replace fluid and eat food the gut already knows. Start with a drink that has some sodium, plus water. Then familiar carbohydrates and some protein, in a portion they can finish without getting sick. Small amounts more often beat one huge meal if the stomach has been empty.

Do not invent an IV, a medical rehydration protocol, or a gram-by-gram schedule from a stored weight. Do not tell them to keep dehydrating because the fight is tomorrow. The cut is over.

If they feel faint, confused, cannot keep fluids down, have chest pain, or have a headache that is not ordinary thirst, that is medical help. Do not coach them through it in the chat.

The training side of fight week is the taper: less volume, some sharp skill, no hard sparring. The meal is not a new experiment and not a celebration that wrecks the next morning. Keep it boring and salty enough to hold the fluid.
