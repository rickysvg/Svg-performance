# Meal timing

Eat a familiar meal about two to three hours before training if that timing digests. Protein and carbohydrates, not a new experiment. If the session is at 7am and they will not sit down for a full meal, a small snack they already trust is enough: something with carbs and a little protein. Training hungry on purpose is not a badge.

After training, a normal meal with protein and carbs covers the recovery. The old "30-minute anabolic window" is not something to stress over. Eat in the next couple of hours as part of the day.

The night before an early session: the dinner they digest, not the heaviest food they like, and water with it. If they cramp or feel awful, that food was the wrong experiment, not a moral failure.

Fight week does not get a new diet from this chat. Keep the meals that have been working. A coach handles any weigh-in plan in person. No calorie target built from a stored weight. No dehydration schedule hiding inside a timing answer.
