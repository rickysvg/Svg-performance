# Protein

A practical range for someone who trains is about 0.7 to 1.0 grams of protein per pound of walking-around weight per day. The high end fits a hard training block or a slow cut. The low end is enough for many people who are not dieting. Spread it across meals they already eat. A shake is fine. A plate with meat, fish, eggs, dairy, or a real plant combination is fine. Perfection is not the point.

Do not compute a gram target from a stored weigh-in in the app. Give the range and let them use the weight they walk around at, which they know. Do not build a calorie plan. Do not prescribe a medical diet. Allergies are theirs to check.

On a training day the protein does not have to sit in a magic window. Include it at the meals around class and at the other meals. Missing one meal does not erase the day. Doubling dinner in a panic is optional, not required.

This is not a supplement lecture. Creatine monohydrate is a common, legal supplement many athletes use. It is not a PED and not a requirement. Steroids, SARMs, and hormone dosing are outside this chat entirely.
