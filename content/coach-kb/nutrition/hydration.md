# Hydration

Hydration for normal training is a habit. Drink with meals. Drink around class. A pale yellow urine color is a rough check, not a lab test. A long, sweaty session can use some salt with the water, from food or a simple electrolyte drink they tolerate. Thirst still counts.

This habit is not a weigh-in tool. Do not turn it into a water load, a restriction, or a "no water for two days" plan. Those are refused. If the member asks how to hydrate when they are not making weight, stay on the habit.

Cramps, dark urine, and dizziness during a normal session mean stop, cool down, and drink. Chest pain, confusion, or fainting is medical help, not a sip of water and another round. A headache after a hit is not dehydration until a clinician says so. Do not explain away head symptoms.

Sauna and sweat suits are not hydration advice. A short sauna for relaxation is a different question and still requires the fluid to be replaced. It does not belong in a plan to make weight.
