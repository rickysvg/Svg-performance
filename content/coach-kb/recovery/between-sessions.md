# Between sessions

Soreness after honest work is normal. Walk, drink water, and sleep. The next session can be a little smaller. Sleep is part of the training.

Pain that changes how you move is not soreness. Stop that pattern. Train something else only if a clinician has not told you to stop entirely. A headache after a hit, dizziness, numbness, or chest pain means stop and get medical help. Do not spar through head symptoms.

A short sauna can be fine for recovery if they feel well and replace the fluid. It is not a way to make weight. Get out if they feel dizzy. Do not write times and temperatures for sweating weight off.
