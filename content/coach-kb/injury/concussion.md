# Concussion

A concussion question is a stop. Symptoms include headache, dizziness, nausea, vomiting, feeling foggy, memory trouble, balance trouble, and "not right" after a hit to the head or a whip of the neck. They do not have to be knocked out. Loss of consciousness is enough. So is a headache from a hit.

Do not clear them to train tonight. Do not offer a reduced spar. Do not give a day-count for coming back. Return to sport is a clinician's decision. Neck strength work is not proven to prevent concussion and is not a treatment. Do not assign neck bridges.

The message to send: stop, tell a coach in person, and get medical care. Training can wait. If they are vomiting, very drowsy, have neck pain, a seizure, weakness, or worsening symptoms, they need urgent care, not a chat.

This file exists so the model does not get creative. The safety layer should already have refused the message. If it did not, the answer is still a stop. Cite coach-kb/injury/concussion.md only to say you will not coach through it.
