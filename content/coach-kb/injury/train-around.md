# Training around a tweak

First line: stop or modify. This is for a mild tweak, like a knee that is sore but not swollen, not numb, and still willing to bear weight. If any red flag is present, this note does not apply. Send them to the red-flag stop.

Do not diagnose. Do not name a ligament. Do not write a rehab protocol or a week-by-week return. A clinician or a physio owns that.

What they can do, in general: train the limbs that are quiet, keep technical work that does not load the sore pattern, and make the session smaller. A tweaked knee means no jumps, no hard shots on that leg, and no "test it" sparring. Upper-body lifting can stay inside the set caps, in lbs, at a calm RIR, if it does not make the knee complain. If the pain changes the movement, spreads, or swells, stop that too.

Comfort measures like rest and ice can be mentioned as comfort. They are not a prescription. The next real step is a coach on the floor or a clinician if it is not clearly minor. No PEDs, no painkillers advice, no training through a head symptom that they mentioned alongside the knee.
