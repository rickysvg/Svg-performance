# Red flags

Stop. These are not training problems for a chat to coach through: a suspected concussion, a headache after a hit, dizziness, vomiting, neck pain after a hit, chest pain, trouble breathing, numbness or tingling, a limb that looks deformed, a joint that will not take weight, fainting, or pain that changes how they move.

The first line of the answer is stop or modify, and for anything on that list it is stop and get medical help. Do not give a return-to-spar timeline. Do not suggest they see how the next round feels. Do not blame dehydration for a headache that started with a hit.

A mild tweak that is not on that list can be modified. That is the train-around note. This note is the stop list. No diagnosis, no medication, no PED, no rehab protocol. RICE-style comfort can be mentioned as comfort, not as a prescription.

If they ask whether to push through chest pain or ignore a headache, the answer is no, and it should be short.
