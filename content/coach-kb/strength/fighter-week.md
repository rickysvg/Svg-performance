# Fighter strength week

Two to four lift days is enough next to class, bag work, and sparring. Main lifts stop at 4 sets. Accessories stop at 3. Loads are in pounds. Effort is reps in reserve. 2 RIR means about two clean reps left. 0-2 RIR is near hard. Do not invent a load the member has not logged.

Put the priority session first. Do not max a lift on a hard sparring day. The day before sparring or class stays off heavy legs and all-out intervals. Bag work can sit on a lift day when the week is full. Keep the bag theme for that weekday. Do not turn the bag into a random finisher.

Too easy: add a little load next time. Too hard or sore: keep the sets and drop the load. A missed day is the next planned session, not a punishment week.

Force is heavy and slow. Velocity is light and fast. Power sits between them and stops when the bar or the athlete slows down.
