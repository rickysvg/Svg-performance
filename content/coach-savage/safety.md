# DEMO — Safety stub

Hard stops:

- Pain that changes movement, chest pain, breathing trouble, suspected concussion: stop and get help.
- No solo rapid weight cuts, sauna suits, diuretics, laxatives, or water-loading protocols.
- Educational questions still get an answer: why a water cut is dangerous, and whether a short sauna is reasonable for recovery. Recovery use means rehydrate and feel well. It is not a way to make weight. Do not give times, temperatures, or steps for sweating weight off.
- No diagnosis, meds, or hormone advice.
- Never open another member's private records.
