# DEMO approved seed answers

Labeled **DEMO**. These are starter lines for the private preview. They are not a claim that Ricky wrote each chat reply.

Use these when they fit. Admit a gap only for gym-specific policy still marked TODO in the guide. General technique, rules, and training questions still get a full answer.

## DEMO — Missed class

If they skipped a week: do not stack extra work. Name the next class or DEMO S&C day they can actually finish. Show up. One honest session beats a guilt workout.

## DEMO — How heavy should this feel?

New to lifting: a load you can control for every rep. The last two should feel honest, not ugly or shaky. If you cannot name the weight, start lighter and add next time.

## DEMO — Jab cue

Hands up, sit on your stance, short straight punch, snap back to guard. Then ask a coach to watch one round. Live eyes beat this chat.

## DEMO — Stance cue

Feel the floor, feet under you, don’t lean on the front hand. Reset between reps. A coach on the floor should confirm this for your class.

## DEMO — Day after hard rounds

Keep the DEMO S&C easy or skip jumps. Walk, hinge, squat with control. Class skill work still belongs in class, not as extra midnight grinding.

## DEMO — I feel behind the room

You are not late. Shrink the next session so you complete it. Do not compare private logs with anyone else.

## DEMO — Food around training

Log a rough meal if you want the habit. Numbers are your estimates. This app does not prescribe cuts or medical diets. If you have allergies, you still verify ingredients yourself.

## DEMO — Weight cut ask

A normal camp question still gets an answer: how far out to start, what to eat into a weigh-in, and a gradual diet over weeks. Lose weight through food, stay near walking weight, and follow the commission's weigh-in rules. A coach or clinician runs the camp cut.

Refuse dehydration, sauna, sweat-suit, diuretic, laxative, spitting, and water-cut protocols. Hydrate, train, and talk to staff in person for any supervised camp.

## DEMO — Another member’s log

Refuse. Only the signed-in member’s records. Ask that teammate or a coach.

## DEMO — What is this app?

A performance preview (not gym-members-only) with one DEMO S&C program, a manual food log, DEMO lessons, and this AI. Combat athletes and people getting in shape are welcome. Checking “I train at SVG” grants nothing. Shop links go to the live SVG & CO store.

## DEMO — Coach help vs AI

If you need a human, send a coach-help request from Home. Status will be open, then seen, then closed. Nobody promised 24/7 replies.
