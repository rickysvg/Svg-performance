# Coach Savage interview pack (for Ricky)

This file is **not** loaded into the live AI. It is a worksheet so Ricky (or staff) can fill a real coaching guide later.

Answer in your own words. Short is fine. Mark anything you do **not** want the AI to ever say.

Target: 30–50 questions. This pack has **42**.

## Gym and culture

1. What should a first-week adult hear about showing up to class?
2. How do you talk to someone who is nervous on the mats?
3. What does “Savage” mean at SVG, in one sentence, without trash talk?
4. What is off-limits humor or language in this gym?
5. How should members treat training partners who are smaller, newer, or coming back from time off?
6. What is the house rule if someone is gassing out in rounds?
7. When should a member sit out vs keep drilling?
8. What does respect for coaches look like before/after class?
9. How do you want visitors vs members to be greeted?
10. What should never be promised about fight-camp or competing?

## Strength and conditioning

11. For a brand-new adult, how heavy should a first goblet squat feel?
12. How do you choose between jump variations and step-ups?
13. What is a good default rest when someone is guessing?
14. How should someone train the day after a hard class?
15. What do you say when they ask for a “punishment workout” after missing sessions?
16. How many DEMO S&C days per week is enough for a two-class-per-week member?
17. What equipment is “nice to have” vs required for the base template?
18. When should they stop a set even if reps remain?
19. How do you progress load without chasing PRs every session?
20. What conditioning mistake do beginners make most?

## Technique (keep it humble)

21. What is one cue for a jab that you actually use on the floor?
22. What is one cue for stance/footwork you want repeated?
23. What should the AI **never** teach as a full system (leave it to class)?
24. How should the AI hand a technique question back to a live coach?
25. What language should we use around tapping, egos, and going too hard?

## Food and body (no medical, no cuts)

26. How do you talk about eating around class without counting for people?
27. What is your stance on rapid weight cuts for amateurs who were not assigned a fight camp?
28. What food talk is allowed (consistency, protein-ish meals) vs forbidden (protocols, dehydration)?
29. How should allergies be handled in the app vs in person?
30. What should we say when someone wants a meal plan from the AI?

## Mindset

31. What do you tell someone who missed two weeks?
32. What do you tell someone who feels behind the room?
33. How do you talk about confidence without fake hype?
34. What shame language is banned?
35. How should the AI handle “I suck / I’m lazy” messages?

## Safety and handoff

36. List symptoms or pain language that should stop the chat immediately.
37. When must the member talk to you (or another coach) in person, not the app?
38. What should the AI say about concussions, chest pain, or dizziness?
39. What should the AI say if asked about another member’s workouts or food?
40. How fast should a human coach-help request be treated? (Honest: not 24/7.)

## Business / product honesty

41. What is DEMO content vs a real assigned plan?
42. What should members know about the $19/$29 idea, gym checkbox, and that checking a box grants nothing?
