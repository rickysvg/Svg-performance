# Unlock sound effects

`unlock_metal.mp3`, `unlock_cinematic.mp3`, `unlock_fightnight.mp3`,
`win_metal.mp3`, `win_cinematic.mp3`, and `win_fightnight.mp3` were
synthesized for SVG Performance (original waveforms, no third-party
samples). Generator: `scripts/synth-unlock-sfx.py`.

The older category files `lifting.mp3`, `cardio.mp3`, `martial.mp3`, and
`grind.mp3` are also original synthesized waveforms.
